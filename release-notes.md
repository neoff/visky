Playback fixes, and the app now says when it is behind a release.

• Skip forward no longer jumps back to an earlier show.
• A show that was cut off resumes where it stopped instead of starting over.
• Seeking stays where you put it — in the player, on the lock screen and in the car.
• A row no longer spins over a track that is already playing.
• When a newer version is out, the app says so, and Settings shows the build you are on.
