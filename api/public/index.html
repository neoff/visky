Visky

Your Gateway to Frisky Radio

Visky is a mobile music streaming application that brings the best electronic music from Frisky Radio directly to your mobile device. Stream your favorite DJ sets, radio shows, and curated playlists anytime, anywhere.

🔐 VK.com Authentication Required

To use Visky, you need an active VK.com (VKontakte) account. The app accesses music content from the official Frisky Radio VK group:

https://vk.com/audios-42311167

Visky acts as a convenient proxy - we don't host any music content, we simply provide a beautiful mobile experience for VK.com's Frisky Radio collection.
