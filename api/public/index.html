Join the beta test

Google gates a production listing behind twelve testers who stay on the closed track for fourteen days. That is what this is for — and it is a mutual deal, not a mailing list.

  1. You need a VK account. The archive the app plays lives on VK, and the app signs in there. Without one there is nothing to play.
  2. You send the email address of your Google account. Play adds testers by address and by nothing else, so this is the one thing that cannot be skipped. It goes to one inbox and is never published.
  3. You send back at least one report. Whether everything works, or that some feature does not — and the number of the report you filed, so there is something to look up. One report, from the form on this page.
