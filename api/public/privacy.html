Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 3, 2025

Privacy Summary: Visky Music Player is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data necessary for app functionality and never share your information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Visky Music Player ("we", "our", or "the Application") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. By using Visky, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data Collected Directly

Data Type Purpose Retention Period
VK.com User ID Authenticate your session and access VK.com content 7 days (session lifetime)
Device ID Maintain session state across app restarts 7 days (session lifetime)
Access Token Temporary authentication with VK.com API 7 days (expires automatically)

2.2 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Personal identifiable information (name, email, phone number)
  • ❌ Payment or financial information
  • ❌ Precise geolocation data
  • ❌ Device contacts, photos, or files
  • ❌ Music listening history or preferences
  • ❌ Browsing history or app usage analytics
  • ❌ IP addresses or network information
  • ❌ Crash reports or diagnostic data

3. How We Use Your Information

The minimal data we collect is used exclusively for:

  • Authentication: Verifying your VK.com account credentials
  • Session Management: Maintaining your logged-in state
  • Content Access: Retrieving music content from VK.com on your behalf

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 VK.com (VKontakte)

Visky uses VK.com's authentication system and API to access music content. When you authenticate:

  • You are redirected to VK.com's official authentication page
  • VK.com's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply during authentication
  • We receive only the access token and user ID from VK.com
  • All music streaming happens directly from VK.com's servers

VK.com Privacy Policy: https://vk.com/privacy

4.2 No Other Third Parties

We do not integrate any analytics services, advertising networks, or third-party tracking tools. Your data stays between you, our application, and VK.com.

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Storage Location

  • Mobile Device: Session data stored locally using encrypted MMKV storage
  • Backend Server: Temporary session cache (expires after 7 days)

5.2 Security Measures

  • 🔒 All API communications use HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • 🔒 Access tokens are never logged or exposed in error messages
  • 🔒 Sessions expire automatically after 7 days of inactivity
  • 🔒 Device storage uses platform-native encryption (Keychain on iOS, EncryptedSharedPreferences on Android)

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Data Access and Deletion

  • View Data: You can see your stored User ID in the app settings
  • Delete Data: Log out from the app to clear all local data immediately
  • Server Data: Server-side sessions auto-delete after 7 days or upon logout

6.2 Account Deactivation

To permanently stop using Visky:

  1. Open the app and navigate to Settings
  2. Tap "Log Out" to clear local session
  3. Uninstall the application from your device

All your data will be permanently deleted within 7 days (when server session expires).

7. Children's Privacy

Visky does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age. The app relies on VK.com's age verification system. If you believe a child has provided information to our app, please contact us immediately.

8. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your information to third parties. We may disclose your information only:

  • Legal Compliance: If required by law or valid legal process
  • Security: To protect against fraud or security threats
  • VK.com API: Your User ID is sent to VK.com to retrieve content (as part of normal operation)

9. International Data Transfers

The Application's backend servers may be located in different countries. By using Visky, you consent to the transfer of your minimal data (User ID and Device ID) to these servers. We ensure appropriate security measures are in place regardless of location.

10. Cookies and Tracking

The mobile application does not use cookies. The backend API uses session cookies only for maintaining your authenticated state. These cookies:

  • Are essential for app functionality
  • Expire after 7 days
  • Are not used for tracking or analytics

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Displaying an in-app notification for significant changes

Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. App Store Compliance

12.1 Apple App Store

This privacy policy complies with Apple's App Store Review Guidelines and App Privacy Details requirements.

Data Types Collected:

  • User ID (Linked to user, used for app functionality)
  • Device ID (Not linked to user identity, used for app functionality)

12.2 Google Play Store

This privacy policy complies with Google Play's User Data Policy and Data Safety section requirements.

Data Safety Declaration:

  • ✅ Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
  • ✅ Data is encrypted at rest (device storage)
  • ✅ Users can request data deletion (via logout)
  • ❌ Data is NOT shared with third parties
  • ❌ Data is NOT collected for advertising

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data:

  • Email: Support via app settings
  • GitHub: https://github.com/neoff/visky

We will respond to privacy-related inquiries within 30 days.

14. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for collecting and using your information:

  • Consent: You provide consent when logging in via VK.com
  • Legitimate Interest: Providing music streaming functionality
  • Contractual Necessity: Authentication required to access VK.com content

15. Your GDPR Rights (EEA Users)

If you are in the EEA, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data (User ID, Device ID)
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion by logging out
  • Portability: Receive your data in machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to data processing (results in inability to use app)
  • Withdraw Consent: Log out at any time
