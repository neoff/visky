Playback fixes throughout, and the app now tells you when it is out of date.

Playing
• Skip forward no longer jumps back to an earlier show.
• A show that was cut off resumes where it stopped instead of starting over.
• Seeking stays where you put it.
• A stream that dies mid-show is reported instead of stalling in silence, and the show moves on.
• Tapping a show whose link has expired fetches a fresh one instead of doing nothing.
• A show that cannot be played at all is skipped rather than sat on.
• A tap always starts the show it names, and a row no longer spins over a track that is already playing.

The window
• It comes back where it was left, at the size it was left — including after it moved between displays, where it used to return at half size.

Between your devices
• Handing the sound to another device works again after that device has been signed out and paired anew.
• A tap is passed on even when the other device already names that show.

This release
• When a newer version is out, the app says so, and Settings shows which build you are on.
